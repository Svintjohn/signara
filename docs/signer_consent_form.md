# Signer consent form (DRAFT TEMPLATE)

**Not legal advice.** Have your thesis adviser and your school's ethics review board
(or equivalent) check this, and check it against the Philippine Data Privacy Act
(RA 10173) and your school's rules, before use. Offer it in the participant's
preferred form: written Filipino/English, with a sign language interpreter or a
signed video explanation if they prefer. Do not rely on text alone.

## Project

Researchers: [names, contact]. Adviser: [name, contact].

## What we ask you to do

## What we keep

| | Yes | No |

## Your rights
- Taking part is voluntary. There is no penalty for saying no.
- You can withdraw at any time, and we will delete your data, including landmark
  numbers made from it, within [14] days of your request. Data already in a
  published paper cannot be removed from the paper, but we will remove your name.
- You may ask what we hold about you, and ask for corrections.
- Your data is stored on [where], accessible only to [names], and deleted by [date].
- We will not sell your data or use it for anything not listed here.
- Compensation: [honorarium / none]. Be transparent, and pay fairly if you can.
