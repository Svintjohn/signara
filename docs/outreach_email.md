# Outreach email (DRAFT, edit before sending)

Send to: Deaf organizations, FSL/ASL teachers, Deaf creators. Verify each contact
yourself before sending. Starting points: Philippine Federation of the Deaf (PFD),
the Deaf and sign language programs of universities near you, your school's
Special Education / Inclusion office. Personalize the first line for each person.
Ask your thesis adviser to be cc'd.

---

**Subject:** Request for guidance: student thesis on a sign language learning app

Hello [Name],

We are [Name 1] and [Name 2], students at [School], working on a thesis app called
**Signara**. It helps hearing people learn basic sign language and gives Deaf and
hearing people a simple way to communicate in emergencies and everyday situations.

We are writing because **we are hearing and we do not want to build this without
Deaf people.** We are not asking for money. We would like to ask for:

1. **A review of our content** (about 1 hour, online or in person): about 60 signs,
   short emergency phrases, and a draft "Deaf culture" section. We want you to tell us
   what is wrong, missing, or disrespectful. We will credit you only if you want.
2. **Optional: signing samples.** If you are willing, we would record you signing a
   few words. This is voluntary, covered by a written consent form (attached), and
   you can withdraw at any time.
3. **Honest advice**, including if you think the project should change or stop.

What we have said so far, so there are no surprises:
- The prototype currently covers **ASL**. We know many Filipino Deaf use **FSL**,
  and FSL is our planned next step. We would like your guidance on that too.
- It is **not** a replacement for interpreters. It does not read facial expressions
  or grammar yet.
- Hand-landmark numbers only are stored from recordings, not your face or video,
  unless you separately agree to something else.

If you are interested, we can send a short demo and answer any questions first.
If this is not for you or not the right contact, we would be grateful if you could
point us to someone who might be.

Thank you for your time.

[Names] · [School/Program] · [Email] · [Adviser name and email]

Attachments: consent form (docs/signer_consent_form.md), one-page project summary.
